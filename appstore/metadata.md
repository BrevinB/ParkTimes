## Description

Every wait time. One glance. ✨

ParkTimes gives you live standby waits, park hours, showtimes, and wait forecasts for Walt Disney World, Universal Orlando, and theme parks around the world — wrapped in a fast, beautiful app that feels a little magical.

KNOW BEFORE YOU GO
• Live wait times for every attraction, refreshed every minute
• Park hours with Early Entry and special ticketed events
• Open, down, and refurbishment status at a glance
• Crowd stats: how many rides are open, average and max waits

OUTSMART THE LINES
• Wait forecasts show how lines will change through the day
• Lightning Lane pricing and availability
• Single rider and virtual queue status
• Wait alerts: get notified when a ride drops under your target time

FIND YOUR WAY
• A live park map with every ride pinned and color-coded by wait
• Walking directions to any attraction
• Browse rides by land or by status
• Search everything instantly

MAKE IT YOURS
• Favorite your must-rides and they pin to the top
• Home screen and Lock Screen widgets with your favorites' waits
• Live Activities keep waits on your Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island while you're in the park
• Gorgeous in both light and dark mode

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No accounts. No ads. No tracking. No data collection — ever.

Parks include Magic Kingdom, EPCOT, Hollywood Studios, Animal Kingdom, Universal Studios Florida, Islands of Adventure, Epic Universe, Disneyland, Disneyland Paris, Tokyo Disney Resort, and many more worldwide.

ParkTimes is an independent app and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by The Walt Disney Company, Universal Destinations & Experiences, or any park operator. All park and attraction names are trademarks of their respective owners. Wait time data is provided by the community-run themeparks.wiki API; times are estimates and may differ from official in-park postings.

## Review Notes (paste into App Review notes field)
ParkTimes displays publicly available theme park wait times from the community
API at https://themeparks.wiki. The app is an independent utility and is not
affiliated with any park operator; a disclaimer appears in the app's About
screen and in the description. Park names are used nominatively to identify
the parks the data refers to. No login is required to use any feature.
Location (when granted) is used only to show the user's position on the
in-app map. The app collects no user data.
