Privacy Policy

Effective August 9, 2026

ParkTimes is built to know as little about you as possible. The short version: we don't collect, store, or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, and no tracking.

Data we collect

None. ParkTimes has no user accounts, no analytics SDKs, no advertising SDKs, and no tracking of any kind. We never see who you are or how you use the app.

Data stored on your device

Your favorites, wait alerts, and settings are stored only on your device (and shared with the app's own widget through Apple's App Group storage). They never leave your device and are deleted when you delete the app.

Network requests

To show wait times, the app requests park data from the community-run themeparks.wiki API. Like any internet request, your device's IP address is visible to that service in order to deliver the response. ParkTimes sends no identifying information with these requests — only which park's data it needs.

Location

If you grant location access, it is used solely to show your position on the in-app ride map and to hand off walking directions to Apple Maps. Your location is processed on-device, is never stored by ParkTimes, and is never transmitted to us or anyone else. The app works fully without location access.

Notifications

Wait alerts use local notifications scheduled on your device. No push notification service receives your data.

Children

ParkTimes does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
