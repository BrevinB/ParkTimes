Common questions

Why do wait times differ from what's posted in the park?
Times come from the community-run themeparks.wiki API and are estimates. They usually match the official posted times closely, but can lag by a few minutes.

Why didn't my wait alert fire in the background?
iOS decides when apps may refresh in the background, so background alerts are best-effort. Alerts always fire while the app is open.

Does the app need my location?
Only if you want to see yourself on the ride map or get walking directions. Everything else works without it.
